~ how our cotton is made ~

From Boll to Stitch

01 / picked

Raw Cotton

Fluffy white bolls are harvested from the field and gently ginned to separate pure natural fiber from seed.

02 / spun

Combed & Spun

Fibers are aligned, combed smooth, and twisted into strong, even yarn with zero synthetic fillers.

03 / stitched

Woven for You

Knit into breathable fabric, cut by hand, and stitched into pieces built to age with character.

Why We Use 100% Cotton zero plastic. zero shortcuts.

No polyester, no synthetic shortcuts. Pure 100% cotton lets your skin breathe, drapes naturally, softens with every wash, and biodegrades cleanly—so what you wear feels honest from the very first stitch.
